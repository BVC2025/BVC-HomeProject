# Leave Policy — BVC24

This document defines how leaves are handled at Bharath Vending Corporation. It is the source of truth for the AI chatbot when answering leave-related questions.

## Types of leave

BVC24 offers four categories of paid leave. Each employee's annual quota is initialised on their joining anniversary and prorated for partial years.

- **Casual Leave (CL)** — 12 days per year. For short-notice personal errands, family events, minor illnesses. Requires manager approval; up to 2 days may be taken back-to-back without medical proof.
- **Sick Leave (SL)** — 12 days per year. For medical reasons. A doctor's note is required when the employee is on sick leave for 3 or more consecutive working days.
- **Earned Leave (EL)** — 18 days per year. Also called Privilege Leave (PL). For planned vacations. Requires manager approval at least 7 working days in advance.
- **Maternity Leave** — 182 days per pregnancy for eligible female employees, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paid in full.

## Casual leave rules

- CL cannot be carried over to the next calendar year.
- Up to 1 day of CL used in a month is considered paid. Any additional CL days may result in loss-of-pay for the excess if the employee is out of balance.
- CL cannot be clubbed with more than 2 EL days at a stretch — for longer breaks, use EL only.

## Sick leave rules

- The first 2 consecutive days of sick leave do not require a doctor's note.
- From day 3 onwards a medical certificate must be attached to the leave application.
- Unused SL beyond the annual quota is not encashable.

## Earned leave rules

- EL up to 18 days may be carried over to the next year. Anything above 18 days lapses on 31st December.
- EL may be encashed at the time of resignation or retirement, up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Half-day EL is allowed.

## Permission (short leave)

- Employees may request **up to 4 hours of paid permission per month** — for banking, medical visits, and similar errands during working hours.
- Permission above 4 hours in a month is unpaid.
- The permission counter resets on the 1st of every month.

## How to apply

1. Open the Employee Portal → Leave section.
2. Choose the leave type, pick dates, add a reason.
3. The system emails your reporting manager, who approves or rejects the request.
4. Approved leaves automatically deduct from the balance and mark the corresponding attendance days.

## Cancellation

- Leaves in PENDING_APPROVAL state can be withdrawn from the Leave page at any time.
- Approved leaves can be cancelled up to the day before the leave start date. After that, they count against the balance.
