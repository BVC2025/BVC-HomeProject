# Attendance Policy — BVC24

## Working hours

- Standard working hours are 09:00 to 18:00, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break.
- Employees on the general shift are expected to be checked in by 09:15. Any check-in from 09:15 to 09:30 is marked as **LATE**. Check-in after 09:30 without prior information is treated as half-day absent.
- Employees on other shifts (Morning / Evening / Night) follow the timings defined for their shift in the Shift Management module.

## Check-in and check-out

- Check-in and check-out happen through the Employee Dashboard → Attendance page. GPS-based geofencing verifies that the employee is inside the office boundary before allowing a check-in.
- If GPS access is unavailable (e.g. the browser blocks location), employees must contact HR to mark attendance manually.
- Forgotten check-outs are auto-closed at midnight and flagged for HR review the next working day.

## Late arrivals

- Up to **3 LATE marks per month** are ignored — they don't affect salary or attendance percentage.
- The 4th LATE within a calendar month counts as **half-day loss-of-pay**.
- The 6th LATE within a calendar month triggers an automatic HR notification for a check-in.

## Absent days

- Any working day without a check-in and without an approved leave is marked ABSENT.
- ABSENT days are loss-of-pay by default. Employees may retroactively apply for CL or SL to convert an absence into a leave, subject to manager approval, within 3 working days.

## Overtime (OT)

- OT starts only after the scheduled shift end time. It requires prior manager approval logged in the OT module.
- OT is paid at 1.5× the regular hourly rate for weekdays and 2× for Sundays and holidays.

## Attendance percentage

- Attendance % is calculated as (Present + Late + Approved Leave) / Working Days × 100.
- The month's target is 95%. Anything below 85% for two consecutive months triggers an HR follow-up.

## Weekly off

- BVC24 follows a **6-day working week**: Monday to Saturday are working. Sunday is the weekly off.
- Public and company holidays are listed in the Holiday Calendar; those override the working-day rule for that date.
