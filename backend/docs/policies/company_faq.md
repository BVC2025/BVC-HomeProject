# BVC24 — Company FAQ

## About BVC24

Bharath Vending Corporation (BVC24) is a Chennai-based manufacturer of vending machines and beverage-dispenser equipment. The company designs, builds, and services machines for offices, factory canteens, hotels, and retail chains across South India.

- Registered name: Bharath Vending Corporation
- Head office: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
- Website: www.bvc24.in
- Business hours: Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 18:00 IST
- Weekly off: Sunday

## Where do I get help?

- **HR questions** (leaves, attendance, salary, personal details) — contact the HR team from the Employee Portal → Contact HR, or email support@bvc24.com.
- **IT / system issues** (login, chatbot, portal errors) — raise a ticket via the HR Automation module.
- **Manager escalations** — every employee has a reporting manager listed on their profile page. If you don't have one assigned yet, contact HR.

## Payroll cycle

- Salaries are processed on the last working day of every month.
- Payslips are available on the Employee Portal → Payslips tab from the 1st of the following month.
- Bank transfer typically reflects in 1–2 working days.

## Reimbursements

- Business-travel, telephone, and internet reimbursements are handled through the Allowances module.
- Submit claims with scanned bills within 7 days of the expense. Approvals go through the reporting manager and then Accounts.

## Where do I find…

- **My leave balance** — Employee Portal → Attendance tab, or ask the chatbot: "How many CL do I have?"
- **My payslip** — Employee Portal → Payslips.
- **My tasks** — Employee Portal → Tasks. Each task shows its due date, priority, and the project it belongs to.
- **The holiday list** — ask the chatbot "Upcoming holidays" or check the Attendance tab.
- **My reporting manager** — Employee Portal → Profile, or ask "Who is my manager?"

## AI chatbot

The BVC24 assistant answers questions about your own data (leave, shift, payslip, attendance, tasks), the holiday calendar, department heads, and BVC24 policies. It runs entirely on the company's own machine — no data is sent to external services.

For anything the assistant can't answer, contact HR directly.
